### UNIT 1: BUDGETING & PLANNING

### OPTIONAL DIGITAL COMPANION — Devon's Month

**Time:** approximately 8 minutes, additional to core instruction; classroom estimate to validate in a pilot.
**When / prerequisites:** after the core workbook work for 1.1 is complete; students should already understand the calculation or reasoning in the connected workbook section (p. 12; optional research p. 13).
**Open:** `budget-builder.html` through the approved Dimension 1 Hub.
**Purpose / what digital adds:** A sequence of events carries earlier budget choices forward through a month.
**DO:** Run Devon’s sample month. Make choices when income and circumstances change, then compare the final plan with the starting plan. If time remains, use Explore This Plan — every line, including income, can be changed to build a full budget from scratch.
**ASK / RECORD:** Name one event, the choice you made, and its effect on the balance. Explain one change you would consider in Devon’s workbook budget.
**Return to:** Apply p. 12 — Devon’s budget.
**Access:** browser on a student/shared device or teacher-projected browser; online access for hosted files, or a tested local copy.
**Privacy and assessment:** use the supplied fictional/sample information. No login, no account, no saved student responses. Copy any wanted reflection before leaving. The workbook and existing assessment remain the evidence of learning.
**No-device route:** complete the existing workbook lesson; omit this optional simulation.
**Control limits:** The story starts with Devon’s sample. Explore This Plan opens pre-filled with Devon’s numbers, but every field, including income, is editable — students can zero everything out and build their own budget from there.

## PART 5: DIGITAL TOOL BEST PRACTICES

### Before Using a Digital Tool

1. **Preview the tool yourself** — Test it before class
2. **Check device availability** — Ensure students can access it
3. **Have a no-device fallback ready** — Always have workbook alternative
4. **Set clear expectations** — What should students record or transfer?
5. **Privacy reminder** — No login, no account, no saved data

### During Digital Tool Use

1. **Model first** — Show students how to use the tool
2. **Set a time limit** — Keep students focused
3. **Circulate** — Check in with students as they work
4. **Ask guiding questions** — Not "right" answers, but reasoning
5. **Connect back** — Transfer findings to workbook

### After Digital Tool Use

1. **Debrief** — Ask what students learned
2. **Transfer** — Have students record key findings in workbook
3. **Assess** — Use workbook evidence, not tool output

A short oral or written observation is sufficient. Do not require a separate duplicate worksheet.

## PART 8: CONCLUSION

Dimension 1 digital companions extend a complete workbook lesson through simulation, branching, comparison, or visualization. They are optional. Students return one observation to the connected workbook work; the workbook and existing assessment remain the evidence of learning.

1. **Visualize** — Show how variables, time, or trade-offs change outcomes
2. **Simulate** — Let students test decisions safely using fictional/sample inputs
3. **Compare** — Organize multiple options or research findings
4. **Plan** — Help students structure a capstone or decision

**Key Reminders:**
- Use the workbook print pathway when digital access is unavailable.
- Use a companion when it strengthens the lesson rather than repeats the same research.
- Use fictional, rounded, or sample information rather than private financial details.
- Transfer useful findings back to the workbook.
