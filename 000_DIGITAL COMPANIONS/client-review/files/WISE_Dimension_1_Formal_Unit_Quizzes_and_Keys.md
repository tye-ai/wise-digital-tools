# WISE Dimension 1 — Unit Quizzes & Answer Keys

**WISE Financial Literacy | Wealth · Income · Saving · Empowerment | A BEAC Initiative | Grades 7–12**

## Administration Notes

- Administer each Unit Quiz after the Unit Review and Vocabulary Review and before the Bridge to the next unit/capstone.
- Each quiz is worth **20 points**.
- Recommended time: **15–20 minutes**.
- Students may use a calculator for calculation items unless local policy says otherwise.
- Do not require private household financial information. All scenarios are fictional.
- A Unit Quiz is a formal checkpoint, not a prerequisite that automatically blocks progression.

# Scoring and Feedback Notes

For calculation items, award method credit only for **student-generated evidence** of the method. A formula, equation, or setup printed by WISE is not student evidence by itself. On Unit 3 Quiz items 1, 3, and 9, a correct final answer earns the full 2 points; an incorrect final answer may earn 1 point only when visible student work demonstrates the correct method with a minor arithmetic error. For reasoning items, score the quality of financial reasoning, not personal disclosure or the student's chosen value. A fictional/example response can earn full credit.

The quizzes are designed from the current Dimension 1 lesson outcomes and formulas but use new scenarios rather than copying Unit Review questions verbatim.
