Devon’s Month

An interactive story where you live through a month with Devon: income arrives, surprises happen, and the choices you make change how the month turns out.

How to open itOpen the Interactive Learning Hub using the access directions on Workbook p. 108, then choose Devon’s Month. Your teacher may use a shared device or project it for the class.
  1. 01Run the monthDevon’s $950 plan is already loaded — nothing to fill out. Tap through the weeks and make a choice when a Money Moment pops up: in Week 2, income comes in $80 lower than planned (cut back, save less, or keep the plan?); later, a $15 weekend decision lands (use what’s planned, add it, or go free?).
  2. 02Watch what changesAfter every choice, the budget updates in front of you. Watch two numbers: Available — what the plan can still cover — and the $100 laptop savings. Did your choice protect them, shrink them, or leave them alone?
  3. 03Explain one consequenceFinish the End of Month check-in, then say it in your own words: one thing your choice changed. Return to Workbook p. 12. Note one event or decision, its effect on the balance, and one change you would consider in Devon’s budget. If time remains, use Explore This Plan to try a different choice or number.
Where this fits
Use after workbook p. 12 Apply — about +8 minutes. Optional: the lesson is complete without it.
No login. No account. Nothing to install. Devon’s numbers are fictional practice values — this is not graded, and nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere.
